ROUNDING-ONLY COMPATIBLE SPEED BANDS
Smart: 0.596774194-0.597222222 pixels/frame.
Tough: 0.620437956-0.622377622 pixels/frame.
These are pairwise feasibility bands assuming constant speed and less than one pixel
of rounding difference between endpoint pairs, not statistical confidence intervals.
The unconstrained least-squares fit minimizes squared residuals; it does not enforce
that hard rounding constraint, which is why Smart's fit lies outside its band.

INTERPRETATION
Smart 7/12 fits worse than its original rate; the measured endpoint fit is faster.
Tough 5/8 fits better than its original rate; the measured endpoint fit is slower still.
These observations measure pointer travel, not a change in midpoint-fix frequency.
Debug midpoint scheduling deliberately targets x=127 with floored travel, while the
accepted midpoint window is x=128-129, so accurate speed can still require adjustment.
