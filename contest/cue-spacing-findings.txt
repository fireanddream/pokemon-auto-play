CPUJ contest cue-spacing survey — completed 20-combination results

Scope and settings
- Requested: one Dance 1 observation for each of 5 categories x 4 ranks.
- First party Pokemon; no dance buttons or stylus inputs.
- Uncapped execution; 7,200 total / 300 per-stage host-second limits.
- Native screenshots add zero emulator frames. Observe every frame during
  Stage 5 and reload the run-owned native slot-0 baseline at the first readable
  Dance 2 entry, including after the last selected target.
- Source runs: spacing-one-dance-20-01 (17 completed observations), then
  spacing-tough-last-three-01 (Tough Super/Hyper/Master).

Totals
- 20 successful Dance 1 observations, one per combination; 1 earlier enrollment
  failure, resolved after the user corrected the prepared Pokemon's eligibility.
- 75 distinct matching cue pairs: 3 per Normal target, 4 per other target.
- 16 measured combinations use 128 pixels; 4 use 120 pixels.
- All 8,716 monitored Stage 5 frames had zero buttons and zero stylus touches.
- All 20 traces were consecutive within one load epoch, stopped on their first
  confirmed Dance 2 frame, and recorded a subsequent baseline reload.
- No dance response inputs were attempted. Excellent/miss/feedback rates do
  not apply to this input-free survey.

Method and direct visual verification
- Independently scan native screenshots for left cue sprites and right rings.
  Calculate matching-label coordinate differences without a configured offset.
  Use complete cue sets on both sides: 3 for Normal, 4 for the other ranks.
- Every first-response screenshot supplied its complete paired cue set.
- Partial cue views are not independent spacing measurements; repeated labels
  in incomplete views can produce misleading alignments.
- Also manually inspected the four 120-pixel cases using the original PNGs
  and 4x nearest-neighbor cue-strip enlargements with a generic native-x ruler.
  The ruler view performs no cue recognition or spacing fitting.

Conclusion and limits
- The 20 measured combinations support category/rank-specific 120/128-pixel
  spacing. A single universal 128-pixel setting is not supported.
- Each completed target has one Dance 1 observation. This survey does not
  establish button timing accuracy, later-dance spacing, or contest win rates.
- Spacing analysis and enrollment-failure review are complete. This aggregate
  report was verified against all 20 observations before working-evidence cleanup.
- Working captures, raw records, and temporary tools were removed after confirming
  inactive owners and restored temporary slot storage. Cleanup left project tmp/
  empty and .tmp/ absent; this report is the retained results artifact.
