# Buyable Poffin plans

[简体中文](能买的宝芬攻略.md)

For a fresh Pokémon with **0 contest stats / Fullness**. Feed Mild **last**.

## Nature guide

**Columns = increased/liked; rows = decreased/disliked.** Diagonal natures are neutral.

| Decreased ↓ / Increased → | Cool / Spicy | Beauty / Dry | Cute / Sweet | Smart / Bitter | Tough / Sour |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cool / Spicy | Hardy | Modest | Timid | Calm | Bold |
| Beauty / Dry | Adamant | Bashful | Jolly | Careful | Impish |
| Cute / Sweet | Brave | Quiet | Serious | Sassy | Relaxed |
| Smart / Bitter | Naughty | Rash | Naive | Quirky | Lax |
| Tough / Sour | Lonely | Mild | Hasty | Gentle | Docile |

## Non-neutral nature

**L** = liked; **D** = disliked; **N1–N3** = distinct neutral flavors.
With Mild and neutral Spicy, set **N1 = Spicy**; otherwise assign neutral labels freely.

| Plan | N2 + N3 | D + N1 | D + L | L + N1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Mild; Spicy liked | 5 | 4 | 2 | 1 |
| Mild; Spicy disliked | 5 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| Mild; Spicy neutral | 5 | 3 | 3 | 1 |
| No Mild | 5 | 3 | 3 | 2 |

## Neutral nature

**N1–N5** = the five distinct flavors. With Mild, **N1 = Spicy**; without Mild, assign labels freely.

| Plan | N2 + N3 | N4 + N5 | N1 + N4 | N1 + N5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| One Mild last | 5 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| No Mild | 5 | 2 | 3 | 3 |
